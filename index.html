Stat range scores are calculated across the full range, including negatives: for most stats, −10 = 0%, 0 = 50%, and +10 = 100%. Speed uses −5 = 0%, 0 = 50%, and +5 = 100%. Priority rank 1 receives the most weight; weights use inverse-rank weighting and are normalized to 100% across the pet's prioritized stats.
